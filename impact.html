Financial Close, Consolidation & CXO Dashboards

Contetra
15 days → ½ daymonthly close, 21 branches in 15 states
Problem
Trial balances arrived in different formats from 21 branches in 15 states. Closing took about 15 days, and leadership saw totals without the reasons behind them.
Built
A tool that maps any trial balance, consolidates with intercompany and branch eliminations and produces financial statements with notes, plus CEO, CFO, CXO and HOD dashboards.
Result
Close fell from ~15 days to under half a day. Built alone in under a month, and the tool scaled to multiple clients.

DRS Fraud Prevention & Settlement

Recykal
58fraud scenarios across 5 detection areas
Problem
A Deposit Return Scheme refunds money for every container returned. Fake or repeated returns drain consumer deposits, and mixing deposits with fee income breaks the books.
Built
Reactive and proactive fraud controls, a transaction simulator, wireframes for the Exception Hub, Entity 360 and SLA monitor, and settlement logic that treats deposits as liabilities.
Result
Fraud, settlement and collection logic defined end to end, plus a time-bound, prioritised plan for the Moldova tender bid.
