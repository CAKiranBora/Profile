Beyond Work

The Person Behind the Profile

Work tells you what I do. This shows the habits behind the work.

Artist: Portraits & Stippling

100+ sketches · WICASA winner

Sold 100+ detailed human portrait sketches. Practised stippling, a technique that needs patience and precision. Won First Prize at the WICASA Art Competition, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

Art taught me that strong outputs come from sustained, detail-oriented effort. The same patience helps when tracing the root cause of a broken finance process.

Self-Builder: Personal Finance App

Built from scratch

Built a personal finance health tracker app independently, not for work, just to learn and solve a personal need. Designed the data model, logic, and interface from scratch.

Also built internal automation tools and utilities used in Contetra's delivery toolkit. When a problem repeats, I prefer to build a reusable system around it.

Teaching, Faculty & Curriculum Author

1,700+ Trained · ICSI Faculty · ICAI Study Circle

Trained 1,700+ professionals across AI tools, Advanced Excel, and Power BI, including 100+ Chartered Accountants taught at an ICAI study circle. Faculty for ICSI's AI4CS programme for Company Secretaries (400+ CS at the Indore conference, 100+ CS in Thane), having delivered multiple sessions and served as a Judge at ICSI AI4CS, Mumbai, evaluating participants' AI-built solutions. Built the full AI for CS Masterclass: 13 modules, live demos, hands-on exercises and practice company files. Authored comprehensive finance and Smart Investment study materials and curriculum for a Singapore-based wealth firm.

I teach and write curriculum because breaking complex finance and wealth frameworks into clear, structured modules helps people use them, from corporate AI upskilling to institutional sessions with ICAI and ICSI. Authored end-to-end investment study material for a Singapore company, and regularly mentor professionals on AI & analytical execution.

Achiever: Built on Preparation

CA Final · 1st attempt

CA Final in 1st attempt. 5th Rank in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. Star Performer and Best Article awards during articleship. Promoted from Credit Analyst to ARM at IDFC FIRST Bank in April 2024. HSC: 93.08%.

There is a pattern: set a high bar, meet it, document the method, then raise it again. Exemption in Economic Laws in CA Final and 100/100 in Sanskrit in SSC point to consistency and preparation.

Competitive Athlete

District level · 3 sports

District-level competitor in Yoga, Tennikoit, and Athletics. The discipline, consistency, and pressure management that competitive sport builds show up in every high-stakes project deadline.

Competing at district level requires showing up, doing the work, and performing under pressure consistently. The same discipline helps during late pre-go-live checks and high-stakes deadlines.
