Capabilities

What I Do

Four things I do: design products, build with AI, fix finance and controls, and run execution for leadership.

01 / Product

Product Management & Architecture

Translate complex business ideas into structured PRDs, user stories, UX wireframes, swimlane process flows, and database schema mappings.

Product ManagementDRSPRD & User StoriesProcess Mapping
  • Lead end-to-end product discovery, research, and MVP scoping for DRS and client products.
  • Build swimlane process flowcharts that proactively identify dead ends and logic gaps before development.
  • Map business process steps directly to Database tables, columns, and codebase dependencies.
  • Generate complete PM stacks: PRDs, User Stories, Acceptance Criteria, and QA Test Cases.
02 / AI & Tooling

Building with AI

Turn business logic into working tools with AI coding agents, and test them against the business rules before anyone relies on them.

Claude CodeMCPAI-assisted CodingAutomation
  • Built the Financial Close and Consolidation Tool and AI assistants for consulting delivery.
  • Built Flowchart Studio (business step to screens, tables and code) and TestKit (role-based checks with no AI judgement).
  • Built working product modules with AI-assisted coding for client pitches, and fixed DRS platform bugs myself.
03 / Risk & Finance

Finance, FP&A & Fraud Controls

Design finance flows, automated FP&A models, fraud prevention and reconciliation that keep the books audit-clean, from month-end close to DRS settlement.

FP&AFractional CFOFraud DetectionDRS FinanceReconciliation
  • Fractional CFO work: build automated, driver-based financial models for SaaS, trading and e-commerce businesses (forecasts, budgets, unit economics, working capital, scenarios and fundraising runway), refreshed from a data folder into Power BI.
  • Design DRS fraud prevention and settlement logic: refunds, retailer fees, unclaimed deposits and clawbacks, with deposits held as liabilities.
  • Consolidate multi-branch books with intercompany and branch eliminations, and reconcile after ERP go-live.
  • Implement automated close mechanisms (Schedule III notes to accounts) and post-ERP data validation.
  • Set up internal financial controls, approval matrices, and exception monitoring loops.
04 / Leadership

Founder's Office & Operations

Drive CXO priorities, operating cadences, PMO structures, Power BI MIS dashboards, and cross-functional leadership across teams of 20+.

Founder's OfficePMO FrameworkPower BI MISOperations
  • Set up PMO structures, WBS, operating rhythms, and governance trackers for enterprise founders.
  • Build CXO MIS dashboards (Power BI, Power Query) that drill down from KPI to ledger for variance and performance tracking.
  • Lead cross-functional execution across product, engineering, finance, and operations teams.
  • Redesign legacy operational workflows (e.g. billing cycle cut from 25-30 days to 7).
